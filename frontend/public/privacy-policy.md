## 1. Introduction

DocumentGem ("we", "our", "us") operates the Document Intelligence Platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- Email address and authentication credentials (managed via Supabase Auth)

### 2.2 Documents You Upload
- Business documents (PDFs, images, DOCX files) that you upload for processing
- Extracted text, metadata, and AI-generated insights from those documents

### 2.3 Chat Data
- Questions you ask through the chat interface
- AI-generated responses

### 2.4 Integration Data
- Google account OAuth tokens (when you connect Gmail or Google Drive)
- Documents automatically fetched from connected services

### 2.5 Usage Data
- Log data, IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- To provide document analysis, categorization, and insight extraction
- To power the AI chat interface using your document context
- To sync documents from connected integrations (Gmail, Google Drive)
- To improve service reliability and performance

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 OpenAI
Your document text is sent to OpenAI's API for classification, extraction, and chat responses. OpenAI processes this data under their [API Data Usage Policy](https://openai.com/policies/api-data-usage-policies). OpenAI does not use API data to train their models.

### 4.2 Supabase
We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Your data is stored in Supabase's infrastructure.

### 4.3 Google APIs
When you connect Gmail or Google Drive, we access your data through Google's APIs in compliance with Google's API Services User Data Policy.

## 5. Data Retention

- Your documents and data are retained for as long as your account is active
- Deleted documents go to trash and can be permanently deleted at your discretion
- You may request complete data deletion by contacting us

## 6. Data Security

- All data in transit is encrypted via TLS
- Authentication is managed through Supabase Auth with JWT tokens
- Data access is scoped per-user through Row Level Security policies
